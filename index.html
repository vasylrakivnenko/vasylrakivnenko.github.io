I’ve actually run a business — not just advised one.

What I build

Six layers decide whether AI survives contact with production.

Nearly every enterprise AI project that fails, fails in the same place: the gap between a demo that impresses a room and a system that runs on a Tuesday morning without anyone watching. These are the layers that gap is made of. I build all six.
