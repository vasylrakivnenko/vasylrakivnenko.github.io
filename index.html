Legal

Jury-instruction drafting from a filed court checklist

Design and build, end to end · law firm, Los Angeles

Before an eviction trial both sides file a checklist of the jury instructions they want read — 15–25 per case, each one full of blanks a paralegal retyped by hand from the complaint, down to which verb agrees with how many defendants there are. The app reads the filed checklist, fills what it can from a 96-template library, and returns a Word draft that highlights every value it filled in yellow and every blank it couldn’t in red. It never picks which instructions apply, and it never hides a gap behind a plausible guess.

A 22-instruction case drafted in about a minute. 70 of the 96 templates need no model call at all.
