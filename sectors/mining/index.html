Where AI creates the most value

01

Predictive Maintenance

Vibration, temperature, and operational telemetry used to predict failures 48–72 hours ahead. Stop replacing parts on a calendar — replace them when the data says to.

02

Ore Grade Optimisation

Models on real-time grade data that dynamically adjust blending and processing parameters, improving recovery without additional throughput.

03

Safety & Compliance AI

Analyse shift reports, near-miss logs, and inspection records to surface patterns before they become incidents — and generate compliance reports from structured operational data.

04

Knowledge Capture

Retrieval systems that preserve the decision-making logic of senior operators, making it searchable for the next generation of your workforce.
